IMAS Data Mapping workshops

Agenda and presentation material of the workshops on experimental data mapping to the IMAS Data Dictionary.

These workshops bring together people working on data mapping in IMAS and experts in fusion experimental data from different devices and multi-machine databases. The mapping status for each device, as well as notes from the regular online meetings, are kept in the IMAS-Data-Mapping repository and its wiki.
